Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026 · Everyday Vet LLC

Everyday Vet LLC (“Everyday Vet,” “we,” “us”) explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit everyday.vet, use our veterinary services (including virtual consults and house calls), or communicate with us by SMS/text or email.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us (name, email, phone number, pet information).
  • Appointment and care details needed to provide veterinary services.
  • Message content and metadata when you text with Everyday Vet (including delivery status and opt-in/opt-out records).
  • Website analytics that may include IP address, pages viewed, and approximate location.

How we use information

  • To provide veterinary care and schedule appointments.
  • To send appointment reminders, care follow-ups, and other service texts you opt into.
  • To respond to questions and support requests.
  • To protect security, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages from Everyday Vet, we (and our messaging providers, which may include Twilio and our IT partner Dorky Robot LLC operating Kita/levee on our behalf) process your phone number and message data to deliver those texts and honor your preferences.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out; reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to text messaging is not a condition of receiving veterinary care or making a purchase.
  • We do not sell your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We share information with service providers that help us operate (hosting, practice software, and SMS providers), when required by law, or with your direction. We do not sell personal information.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all sharing described above. This information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
