About Everyday Vet LLC

Everyday Vet LLC is a modern, client-centered veterinary practice dedicated to making high-quality pet care more accessible, convenient, and stress-free. Our primary focus is on virtual consultations available to pet owners throughout the state of Ohio, allowing you to receive expert guidance from the comfort of your home—without the hassle of travel, waiting rooms, or added anxiety for your pet.

When an in-person examination or hands-on procedure is necessary, we offer select in-person house calls in the greater Cleveland area. This hybrid model ensures your pet gets the right level of care exactly when and where it's needed, prioritizing their comfort and your convenience.

We emphasize compassionate, evidence-based medicine with a strong commitment to client education, clear communication, and reducing barriers to veterinary care. Whether through a quick virtual consult or a carefully scheduled house call, Everyday Vet LLC is here to support you and your pet every step of the way.

Dr. Sara Meiggs, DVM

Veterinarian & Founder

Dr. Sara Meiggs is a licensed veterinarian who graduated from The Ohio State University College of Veterinary Medicine in 2017. With nearly a decade of clinical experience, she founded Everyday Vet LLC to bring accessible, low-stress veterinary care directly into clients' homes and through virtual platforms.

Dr. Meiggs has a special interest in behavioral medicine and comprehensive patient management, including tailored diet selection, preventive care, and overall pet welfare. She is deeply passionate about client education and bridging the communication gap between veterinary professionals and pet owners, empowering families to make informed decisions for their companions.

Dr. Meiggs lives in Cleveland with her husband, young daughter, and their cat, Chaplin (named for his distinctive mustache). Her personal commitment to family, animals, and community drives her dedication to providing thoughtful, individualized care for every patient she sees.

We look forward to partnering with you to keep your pet healthy and happy.
