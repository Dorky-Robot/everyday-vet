SMS Opt-In

Public page for appointment & care texts · Everyday Vet LLC

Everyday Vet LLC can text you about appointments, reminders, and care follow-ups. Two-way SMS for scheduling and care coordination — not marketing.

How to get appointment & care texts

  1. Text START to (216) 999-5420, or give the clinic your mobile number during intake.
  2. You will receive one confirmation text from Everyday Vet LLC.
  3. Reply YES to agree. Until you reply YES, no other texts are sent.

By replying YES you agree to receive recurring automated and staff text messages from Everyday Vet LLC about appointments, reminders, and care follow-ups at the number you texted from. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase or veterinary care.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Privacy Policy: everyday.vet/privacy/ · Terms: everyday.vet/terms/

How opt-in works

Opt-in is collected by text message. End users either text the keyword START to (216) 999-5420, or provide their mobile number to clinic staff during intake. In both cases Everyday Vet LLC sends a single confirmation text containing the program name, message frequency, rate disclosure, and STOP/HELP instructions, and the end user must reply YES before any further messages are sent. The inbound YES is the consent record. Reply STOP at any time to cancel; HELP for help. There is no web form and no pre-checked consent.
